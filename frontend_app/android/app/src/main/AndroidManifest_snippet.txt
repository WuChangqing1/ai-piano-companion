在 Flutter 工程生成后,把下列权限合并到
android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml 的 <manifest> 节点内:

<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET"/>
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.CAMERA"/>
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO"/>
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE"/>

并在 <application> 节点上增加(允许局域网 http):
    android:usesCleartextTraffic="true"

iOS 端在 ios/Runner/Info.plist 中增加:
<key>NSCameraUsageDescription</key><string>需要摄像头录制练琴视频</string>
<key>NSMicrophoneUsageDescription</key><string>需要麦克风录制琴声</string>
<key>NSAppTransportSecurity</key><dict><key>NSAllowsArbitraryLoads</key><true/></dict>
